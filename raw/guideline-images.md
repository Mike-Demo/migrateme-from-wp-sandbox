Visual identity (match the DXS app editions): warm cream/ivory background (#FBF9F3), dark warm-ink tones (#1F1B16), and a single burnt-orange accent (#D9431F). Editorial, magazine-like, understated.

- Prefer flat, diagrammatic graphics and honest screenshots over photography; if photography is used, it must look unstaged and documentary, never glossy stock.
- Avoid: purple/blue gradient startup art, glassmorphism, generic AI-gloss illustrations, and stock-photo handshakes.
- Every image must have descriptive alt text under 125 characters.
- Prefer images uploaded to this site's media library over hotlinked external images: exports must carry their own media.
- Landscape orientation for hero and header images.
